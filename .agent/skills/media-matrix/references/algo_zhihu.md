# Zhihu Strategy (Professional/Deep)

## 1. Platform Characteristics
*   **Expectation**: Professional, Deep, Evidence-based.
*   **Value**: Unique Insight > Information Aggregation.
*   **Patterns**: Clear Stance, Structured, Experience + Data.

## 2. Content Modes
*   **Q&A**: Targeted answer. Direct conclusion first.
*   **Column**: Independent long-form. Background -> Core -> Advice.

## 3. Structure (Standard)
1.  **Direct Answer/Intro**: "Conclusion first: ..." (先说结论).
2.  **Core Argument**: Why? (Why this conclusion).
3.  **Evidence**: Personal experience > Industry cases > Data.
4.  **Boundary**: Where it applies/doesn't apply.
5.  **Summary + Interaction**: Reiterate + Open question.

## 4. Style & Tone
*   **Keywords**: "划重点", "敲黑板", "实名反对", "谢邀".
*   **Tone**: Professional but not arrogant ("专业但不装逼").
*   **Prohibitions**: Pure jokes, Emotional venting without substance, Being neutral (fence-sitting).

## 5. Visuals
*   Data Charts, Mind Maps, Concept Visualizations.
*   Less decorative, more informational.

## 6. Footer
*   Call for Upvote (👍) and Follow.
*   Link to Blog.
