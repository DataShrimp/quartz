This is the singularity for autonomous agents. OpenClaw jumps out of the browser sandbox to gain Mac/Linux TCC permissions. But more importantly: It has a HEARTBEAT (Cron + Webhooks).

- `SOUL.md`: Personality, behavioral constraints.
- `MEMORY.md`: Evolving knowledge log across sessions.
Add the CDP (Chrome DevTools Protocol) + Bash access, and it runs your ops autonomously.
